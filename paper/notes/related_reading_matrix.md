# ALMOND Related Reading Matrix

This matrix summarizes the expanded paper set under `paper/related_papers/` and how each group informs the Introduction and Related Work sections.

| Group | Papers | Main problem addressed | Method family | Limitation relative to ALMOND | How ALMOND uses it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target-guided and proactive dialogue planning | Tang et al. 2019; Qin et al. 2020; Zhong et al. 2021; Kishinami et al. 2022; Yang et al. 2022; Wang et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2023/2024; Zheng et al. 2024; Guo et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2025 | Lead an open-domain or proactive conversation toward a target topic, conclusion, or planned path. | Keyword transition, knowledge routing, KG planning, Brownian bridge planning, LLM planning, reflection/correction. | Usually assumes a fixed target or endpoint rather than a natural-language macro-goal whose short-term skill interpretation changes. | Establishes the dialogue-planning lineage and motivates why ALMOND needs drift-aware skill selection. |
| Target-driven recommendation data and methods | Liu et al. 2020; Liu et al. 2021; Wang et al. 2022; Dao et al. 2024 | Build and plan proactive recommendation dialogues over movies, music, and POI domains. | DuRecDial data construction, action/topic planning, experience retrieval, target-driven recommendation policy learning. | Optimizes recommendation target achievement, but does not directly handle buyer-side negotiation constraints or skill adaptation under intent drift. | Provides the recommendation benchmark foundation and related target-driven planning background for ALMOND scenarios. |
| Negotiation dialogue foundations | Lewis et al. 2017; He et al. 2018 | Learn strategic bargaining policies and separate negotiation strategy from language generation. | End-to-end negotiation learning, self-play/RL, modular strategy and generation, Craigslist bargaining data. | Strong negotiation foundation, but not designed for ambiguous macro-goal control or intent-drift-aware MORL. | Grounds ALMOND's buyer-agent/seller-simulator setting and the use of price/deal/fairness objectives. |
| Adaptive dialogue policy learning | Deng et al. 2023 survey; PPDPP; TRIP; PADPP | Train policy planners or adaptive planners for proactive, non-collaborative, or multi-objective dialogue. | Plug-in policy planning, AI-feedback RL, diversified simulation, multi-objective RL with GPI. | PADPP is the closest ancestor but assumes numeric preference vectors are given; PPDPP/TRIP do not map ambiguous macro-goals to drift-aware skill selection. | Positions ALMOND as extending adaptive dialogue policy learning with hierarchical skill control and strategic hints. |
| Intent understanding and drift | IntentionFrame; DriftHunter | Understand fine-grained conversational intentions and detect intent changes over time. | Semi-structured intention schema; temporal transition accumulation. | Mainly focuses on understanding/detection, not downstream constrained control. | Justifies seller/persona drift, deterministic drift triggers, and the T2DA metric. |
| Dynamic-preference MORL and safe RL | Abels et al. 2019; Yang et al. 2019; Hayes et al. 2022; Barreto et al. 2017; Barreto et al. 2018; Achiam et al. 2017 | Generalize policies across changing objective weights and satisfy constraints. | Dynamic-weight MORL, envelope Q-learning, successor features, GPI, constrained policy optimization. | General RL setting, not dialogue-specific and not controlled by ambiguous natural-language goals. | Supplies the technical background for regret-aware skill learning, reusable policies, and constraint-aware metrics. |

## Writing stance

- Credit PADPP as the closest baseline and direct technical ancestor.
- Group target-driven planning methods as related but not equivalent: they improve target/path planning, while ALMOND studies skill adaptation under intent drift.
- Acknowledge dynamic-weight MORL explicitly; the narrower gap is inferring short-term skills from ambiguous natural-language macro-goals and dialogue history.
- Describe ALMOND claims conservatively as observed benchmark improvements, not universal claims.
- Use percentage-point phrasing for all reported improvements.
